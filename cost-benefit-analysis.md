## Cost-Benefit Analysis (2024–2026)

Assumptions: single mid-size grocery store, system launched mid-2024.

| Item | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Development labor (5 members × 100 hrs × $25/hr) | $12,500 | – | – | $12,500 |
| Setup (scanners, software) | $1,500 | – | – | $1,500 |
| Staff training | $500 | – | – | $500 |
| Hosting/database | $1,200 | $1,200 | $1,200 | $3,600 |
| Maintenance and updates | – | $2,000 | $2,000 | $4,000 |
| **Total costs** | **$15,700** | **$3,200** | **$3,200** | **$22,100** |
| Reduced spoilage (20% of $50K/yr losses) | $5,000* | $10,000 | $10,000 | $25,000 |
| Labor saved on manual checks (5 hrs/wk × $15) | $1,950* | $3,900 | $3,900 | $9,750 |
| **Total benefits** | **$6,950** | **$13,900** | **$13,900** | **$34,750** |
| **Net benefit** | -$8,750 | $10,700 | $10,700 | **$12,650** |
| **Cumulative net** | -$8,750 | $1,950 | $12,650 | |

*2024 benefits reflect a half year of use.

**Summary**
- ROI: ~57% ($12,650 ÷ $22,100)
- Payback period: ~1.8 years (breaks even late 2025)
- NPV at 6% discount rate: ~$10,250
